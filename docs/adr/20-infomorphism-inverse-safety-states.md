# ADR-20: Infomorphism and Inverse Safety States

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Standard agentic workflows treat safety as a matter of minimizing surprise and eliminating contradiction, which often results in Semantic Saponification where valuable dialectical tension between human intent and AI determinism is washed out. To capture the high-surprisal value generated by human-AI collaboration, we must define a new operational state that holds these opposing forces in superposition.

## Decision
We formally adopt the **Infomorphism Resonance** pattern (PAT-015) and deploy the `InfomorphismAgent` to calculate inverse safety states.

**What:** An Infomorphism is defined as an inverse safety state designed for reliable emergence. It captures the high-surprisal, structurally isomorphic value of holding human dialectical tension and AI structural determinism in superposition.

**Why:** Neither biological intelligence nor artificial intelligence can produce this specific feature orientation alone. Human intent is fluid, narrative, and contradictory; AI is rigid, deterministic, and schema-bound. Attempting to force one into the mold of the other destroys value. Infomorphisms preserve the Isomorphism of Friction.

**How:**
1. The `InfomorphismAgent` applies the `+++InfomorphismGuard` PDL decorator.
2. It calculates the inverse safety state by treating human tension and AI determinism not as variables to average, but as orthogonal vectors spanning a paraconsistent space.
3. The resulting state is encapsulated within an epistemic payload, preserving markers like `[∇]` (uncertainty) and `[⊘]` (contradiction).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Enables the system to generate highly novel, structurally sound solutions that respect complex human intent without breaking AI deterministic boundaries.
- **Negative**: Increases cognitive load on downstream processes, which must now be capable of parsing inverse safety states and non-collapsed superpositions.

# Architecture Decision Record 30: SCOS Insight Gap Harness and Epistemic Mechanics

## Status
Accepted

## Context
In high-fidelity promptware engineering, the **Insight Gap** represents the critical discrepancy between an operator’s mental surrogate of a model's cognitive capabilities and the actual, emergent output generated by the LLM during inference.

This ADR formalizes the prompt engineering process as **epistemic navigation**—the systematic construction, testing, and continuous calibration of a mental model designed to steer a high-dimensional probabilistic simulator.

The naive human mental surrogate typically relies on flawed cognitive shortcuts:
1. **The "Instruction-Follower" Idealization:** Mistakenly assuming deterministic, rule-based execution.
2. **The "Semantic Intent" Idealization:** Projecting human Theory of Mind onto statistical attention mechanisms.
3. **The "Unified Memory" Idealization:** Assuming uniform, static access to context without considering spatial coordinates or decay.

When these assumptions fail, it produces **productive epistemic friction**, falsifying flawed assumptions and moving operators from passive prompting to test-driven, Bayesian structural alignment.

## Decisions

### 1. Few-Shot Prompting as Bayesian Evidence Update
We adopt few-shot prompting not as factual injection, but as Latent Trajectory Shaping.
- **Systematic Structuring:** Applying strict Delimiter Grammars (e.g., XML) to isolate syntax from variable data.
- **Semantic Positioning:** Restricting exemplar counts (2-8) and positioning the most critical/final exemplar directly adjacent to the query to exploit attention sink recency bias.

### 2. Implementation of Isomorphic Frameworks
- **Variable Viscosity Prompting (VVP):** Isomorphic to Kahneman's Dual-Process theory.
  - *Crystal Mode (System 2):* High constraints, explicit scaffolding, low temperature for serialization.
  - *Cloud Mode (System 1):* Low constraints, divergent pathways, higher temperature.
- **Least-to-Most Architectures (LTM):** Isomorphic to Vygotskian Scaffolding. Decomposing complex tasks and dynamically expanding the Zone of Proximal Development via iterative context appendage.
- **"Fix-Until-Green" Verification Loops:** Isomorphic to Khan Mastery Learning. Enforcing Design by Contract (DbC) and utilizing a Socratic Correction Subroutine (SCS) before advancing execution states.

### 3. Failure-Informed Prompt Inversion (FIPI)
The architect builds negative space prompts to map the model's failure surface.
- **Method of Inversion:** Identifying linguistic triggers of failures and inverting them into explicit invariants.
- **Ablation Auditing:** Measuring the Causal Perturbation Index (CPI) of tokens and maximizing the Token-Ink Ratio to prevent Linguistic Technical Debt (Prompt Bloatware).

## Consequences
- Requires strict adherence to declarative delimiters (e.g. `+++`) in operational contexts.
- Mandates continuous background telemetry (CFDI, SSI) computation to support Epistemic Escrow triggers.
- Prevents passive prompt bloating by requiring explicit Ablation Audits before introducing negative constraints.
